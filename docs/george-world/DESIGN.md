### What it is + teaching goal (D, 2026-08-29, verbatim)
A **3-D god-game / civilization builder centered on the ECONOMY**, with a very **LIGHT tech tree** (a small, manageable set of skills/tools being built). Reuses the HvB **Georgeomat** machine — Vitas and the connections between them — as its engine, polarity flipped toward **building** a society rather than feeding the dragon. You win by maximizing collective flourishing; you can also just **stop at the village level and run a harmonious village**. (D)

**Teaching goal (D):** a top-grossing, beautiful game that "beats the theory of George into players' heads" so they can **automatically identify georgist dynamics (rent, land, extraction) in the real world** and have opinions about them. Beautiful but **NOT intricately detailed**.

## THE RISE TO POWER must be EARNED, slow, and DRAMATIC (D, 2026-08-30) — keystone, core of the game
Doug on the Nine-Vita Terror: the hierarchy emerges WAY too fast and too AUTOMATIC — "I just started a terror and already there's two levels of hats," even on fast-forward — and the top Vita "just sort of slides into" the top position. He wants to WATCH power be EARNED: **coercion, leaning (pressuring), convincing (persuading), resisting, recruiting** — the pyramid assembled struggle by struggle over real time, deeper and more thorough. This is the beating heart of the game. Two parts:
1. **Slow the emergence dramatically (now):** raise recruiting/clamp thresholds + pace way up so the pyramid takes a LONG time to form; the top earns its position over a long, visible process, not seconds.
2. **The deep recruitment DRAMA (its own focused build):** actual scenes of a Vita pressuring another, the other RESISTING or yielding, alliances forming, someone convinced, someone coerced — the rise to power told as drama, not an automatic threshold clamp. This is exactly what the editable-Dramaton-scenes architecture is for: recruitment dramas become authored/tunable scenes.
- **Recreation gated to HUMANE societies (D):** in an extraction terror the ground-down WORKERS should NOT surf/recreate — recreation is the DIVIDEND of a humane/surplus society. Gate recreation per-Vita on genuine leisure + surplus + low coercion. (An idle boss lounging = conspicuous leisure is fine — pure Veblen — but exploited workers never play.)

## Rank is EARNED, and player-composed setup (D, 2026-08-30)
- **RANK & HATS ARE EARNED, NOT PRESET.** No Vita starts with a rank or a silly hat. Rank (and the increasingly-silly hat) is EARNED/ASSEMBLED through RECRUITING during play — a Vita climbs by recruiting others under it (assembling subordinates/enforcers); its hat gets sillier as rank rises. The hierarchy self-assembles through recruiting; hats appear as rank is earned. (Even the Terror preset starts everyone rankless/hatless and lets the pyramid + hats EMERGE.)
- **Player-composed setup:** the player decides the cast — choose the NUMBER of Vitas (1–9); QUICK-SHAPE each Vita before plugging it in (a lightweight trait picker: generous / hard worker / frugal / greedy… — the trait-composer at setup, e.g. "make them all generous hard workers"); OR pick a PRESET like "THE NINE-VITA TERROR" (auto-roll/configure 9 primed for extreme extraction). Flow: pick count → shape each (or preset) → drop them in → rank/hats/hierarchy EMERGE through recruiting.

## Collapse endgame — extraction → climate change → famine (D asked 2026-08-29)
Doug's question: how complex must George World be to depict the endgame of extreme extraction → climate change → famine; can 64 Vitas do it; can 3 levels? Analysis (C): the gate is MECHANISMS, not Vita count — three pieces not yet built:
1. **Depletable commons** — resources must be exhaustible (harvest faster than regrowth → the grove/fishery COLLAPSES). The tragedy engine. (Currently fruit regrows on a timer, so you can't over-extract.)
2. **Climate feedback** — total production/extraction emits "heat/strain" that past a threshold degrades the whole environment (drought, failing crops, dying fisheries): a rising climate meter that kills the commons everywhere.
3. **Famine consequences** — when the commons collapses, mass starvation; the hoards can't save anyone ("you can't eat gold"). The gut-punch: the extractors devoured what they extracted from — even the king starves.
NUMBERS: you do NOT need 64 — collapse is about RATES (extraction vs regrowth) + FEEDBACK loops, not headcount; a handful demonstrates the causal chain. 64 is worth it later purely for IMPACT (a teeming society collapsing hits harder). THREE levels is plenty (king who hoards / enforcers who squeeze / workers ground against a dying land). Rec: a ~9-Vita, 3-level "collapse" demo (1 king, 2 enforcers, 6 workers) + the three mechanics; then scale the cast to 64 for spectacle. Teaching: extraction doesn't just make rich & poor — it eats the commons everyone stands on; inequality and climate collapse are the SAME machine over-running.

## PACING — slow it WAY down, let each level LINGER (D, 2026-08-30) — HIGH PRIORITY
Doug's biggest note: the sim runs WAY too fast — the whole arc races to the end in a few minutes, discoveries click by before you can take advantage, and you never see it play out or feel a level. Fixes:
- **Slow the base rate dramatically** so the society plays out and the player can watch + act.
- **Space out the tech-tree DISCOVERIES/levels** (raise thresholds / slow accumulation) so the society OPERATES at each level for a good while — long enough to BUILD UP RESERVES or to SUFFER the consequences of that level — before the next unlock. Each level should breathe and matter.
- **Illustrate each discovery's BENEFIT** — when a tech lands the world visibly gets easier at that task, held long enough to SEE the improvement (pairs with the longer discovery scenes).
- **Enrich the teaching with MANY more little SCENES** that (a) narrate a benefit ("this is so much easier now!") and (b) reveal CHARACTER — Vitas commenting on each other, e.g. "Delta is so stingy," "Yucky is so kind and generous." Character + consequence, out loud. (Extends the conversation-variety note.)
- **Pathfinding bug:** Vitas go straight OVER the hill instead of using the PATH over the ridge saddle (the quicker route). Make them prefer/use the path; only crest the hill directly with surplus energy or a specific reason.

## Capitalism as a PONZI SCHEME — a lens (D asked 2026-08-30, explore when rested)
Honest version ("all of capitalism is a Ponzi" overreaches — it produces real value — but the Ponzi-LIKE parts are exactly George's concerns): a Ponzi pays the top from an ever-widening base of new entrants and collapses when new entrants dry up. Three capitalist dynamics ARE that:
- **Speculation** (land / housing / stocks / crypto) — prices rise because a later buyer will pay more, not from real value = George's own land-bubble boom/bust.

States it should act out: hungry · working too hard · producing too much and not eating enough · tummy full and hardly working (full and idle). Because a symbol has to be learned and a little figure clutching its stomach or slumping over a full basket is read by anyone at a glance, which is the "beats the theory of George into players' heads" goal at thumbnail size.

Because the skin gallery means a Vita can look like anything; a read-out re-animated per skin would never stay current, and a doll that looks the same on every Vita is the thing a player learns once.

### 3. Descriptors that play BOTH ways + measuring the model (Doug, 2026-09-02)

### 4. How do we introduce generosity? (Doug asked; Ludo seeded; Doug resolved, 2026-09-02)
Doug's question on the ride. Ludo's seeds, offered to react to (Ludo proposed, 2026-09-02; none adopted as mechanics):
- Vita-to-Vita generosity as emergent behaviour: a Vita with a full basket hands a berry to a hungry neighbour, a small pantomime. Shows people are generous by nature and contrasts with the machine that isn't. (Peer gifts already exist in the game as the trade beat, `tradeGift`, since the trade batch.)
- Generosity-from-above as theater: the King's Sunday distribution is performative charity the beaver notes barely dents the extraction, charity versus justice. (Already the built Sunday sequence and its "THE BLESSING" hint.)
- Generosity as a losing intervention, the Georgist punchline: let the player try to fix poverty with charity and watch the arrangement stay unchanged, against the one clean lever (land tax). Generosity is kind but doesn't touch the choke point. Strongest teaching device; fits the machine thesis. Whether to build it is CANON open question 13.

**RESOLUTION (Doug, same ride):** generosity is ALREADY in the model: the **three-way outflow**. A Vita can direct some of its output (1) upward to the pump, (2) to yourself, or (3) as a gift to a person or the community. So there is no new mechanic to build; the work is to write it into the Vita STATE DESCRIPTIONS, so the read-out says which way a Vita leans:
- gift-heavy → "generous"
- self-heavy → "selfish"
- pump-heavy → "a beautiful taxpayer" (Doug's phrase, verbatim: the extraction dressed as civic virtue; keep it)

Why this beats a new mechanic: the three-knobs rule stands ("Do not add knobs to the Vita machine right now"), and the point of generosity in George World is not that it fixes anything but that the player can SEE who is kind and who is being skimmed; naming the lean on the doll does that with no new machinery. It feeds straight into the state doll of section 2. (C-note for the builder: v0.79 has peer gifts and the tithe pipe, but no per-Vita split of output among pump / self / gift that a descriptor could read off; whether that split is a derived statistic or a knob belongs to the Georgeotron round-trip session, and the three-knobs rule leans it toward derived.)

### 9. Heterogeneous productivity: strong-at-5 vs weak-at-flat-out (Doug asked; Ludo answered; seed accepted 2026-09-02, mechanics TBD)
- Doug's question: Vitas produce different amounts. A strong Vita at effort 5/10 might out-produce a weak Vita flat out. How to model it, and does George address it?
- **George does address it** (Ludo, 2026-09-02): the "wages of superior ability" / "rent of ability". George treats the extra return to exceptional strength or skill as rent-like, a scarcity premium, BUT his thesis is that individual differences are NOT the cause of poverty; land rent capturing the surplus is. Productivity variation is the fair, earned part; the injustice is the unearned skim on top.
- **Model seed (Ludo proposed, 2026-09-02; Doug accepted as a seed, CANON 15):** give each Vita a capacity/strength attribute; output = capacity × effort-knob, measured in berries. Strong at 0.5 beats weak at 1.0 naturally, no special case. Against the three-knobs rule: capacity is an attribute the world sets, not a knob the player turns, like the per-Duke spring-strength trait already in the build.
- **Teaching payoff:** the game can separate the two. A strong Vita producing more by working = fair wages of labour; the Duke producing more by holding the claim captures surplus regardless of who made it = rent, the unearned part. Showing earned-difference next to unearned-skim is pure George: the machine doesn't care who's strong; it skims the claim either way.
