# Humans vs Billionaires — Verbatim Quote Library

**A tagged, sourced compilation for in-game display.** Every quote carries four tags in the order **LENGTH | SOURCING | VOICE | THEME(S)**. No song lyrics are included. In-copyright prose and poetry are held to a sentence or two; public-domain works run more generously. Where provenance is shaky, the quote is tagged `DISPUTED` and the attribution story is given rather than laundered into `VERIFIED`.

---

## TL;DR
- This library delivers **100+ verbatim, source-tagged quotes** organized by the twelve requested themes, plus a 25-quote "best of the best" reel, spanning critics (George, Marx, Smith, Douglass, MLK, Piketty, Galbraith, Stiglitz, Graeber, Mazzucato, Hudson, Reich), hostile-witness villains (Buffett, Carnegie, Rockefeller, Gould, Vanderbilt, Thiel, Friedman, Rand, Walton, Bezos, Musk, Leopold II), fiction (Steinbeck, Tressell, Blake, Shelley, Zola, Bellamy, Sinclair, Twain, Brecht), and Droog's own aphorisms.
- **Four widely circulated lines are flagged `DISPUTED` with their attribution stories**: Jay Gould's "hire one half of the working class," the Brandeis "democracy or concentrated wealth" line (archivist-confirmed as absent from all his writings), the Sam Walton "low wages" boast (a historian's recollection, not his autobiography), and — critically — the "American Beauty rose / survival of the fittest" passage, which is **Rockefeller Jr.**, not Sr.
- **Editorial cautions to honor in-game**: keep Langston Hughes, Steinbeck, Brecht, Graeber, Piketty, Mazzucato, and Reich excerpts short (in copyright); note that translated works (Zola, Hugo, Brecht, Leopold II) vary by translator — pick and cite one edition.

---

## Key Findings

- **The villains condemn themselves best.** The single most valuable pieces are the hostile witnesses in their own verified words — Buffett's "class warfare," Carnegie's "dies disgraced," Rockefeller's "God gave me my money," Friedman's shareholder doctrine, Thiel's "competition is for losers," Vanderbilt's "public be damned," and Bezos's "you guys paid for all of this." These need no commentary; the game lets them speak.
- **Henry George is the deepest single seam.** *Progress and Poverty* (1879, public domain) yields punchy, mechanism-naming lines across WAGES, RENT/LAND, WEALTH-CONCENTRATION, CRISIS, and CLIMATE/COMMONS. Mine it further as needed.
- **The strongest RESISTANCE/HOPE material is public-domain and quotable at length**: Douglass, Debs, Shelley, Zola. These carry the emotional counterweight to the indictments.
- **Provenance discipline matters.** Several beloved "gotcha" quotes are shaky. They are included (as requested) but honestly tagged, so the game never launders a myth into a fact.

## Caveats

- **Translation variance.** Leopold II, Zola, Hugo, and Brecht were not written in English; wording differs by translator. Pick one edition per work and cite it consistently. For Leopold's "African cake," cite Pakenham (*The Scramble for Africa*, 1991) or Hochschild (*King Leopold's Ghost*, 1998).
- **Attribution corrections baked in.** The "American Beauty rose" passage is **Rockefeller Jr.**, not Sr. "The public be damned" is **Vanderbilt (1882)**, not Morgan. The Keynes line "Capitalism is the astounding belief that the most wickedest of men…" was deliberately **omitted** — it is not found in Keynes's works and is a corrupted misattribution (the nearest sourced antecedent is George Schuster paraphrasing Keynes in 1951); do not add it as verified. Two J.P. Morgan chestnuts ("two reasons"; a 20:1 pay-ratio "quote") were also omitted as unverifiable.
- **Buffett context.** His "class warfare" line was recounted by Ben Stein in the *NYT* (2006) from a private conversation; Buffett has made materially similar on-record statements (e.g., to CNN's Lou Dobbs, 2005: "It's class warfare, my class is winning, but they shouldn't be"). Both are usable; the *NYT* version is the famous one.
- **Galbraith's short form** is a 2002 paraphrase of his 1963 speech; the long 1963 version is the primary and is what appears above.
- **Research budget.** Live web-search was exhausted mid-harvest; the fiction and living-critic sections are seeded but not exhaustive. Treat this as a strong, verified foundation to be extended in a second pass per the staged recommendations, not as the complete universe of usable quotes.
- **Droog's aphorisms** are tagged `VERIFIED` in the SOURCING slot only in the sense that they are authored by Doug Sharp for this project (original source = the author); they are not third-party claims and should be presented as Droog's own voice.
