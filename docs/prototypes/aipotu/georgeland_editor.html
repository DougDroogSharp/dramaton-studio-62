How to drive the Georgeland Editor

Tap the ground to place the chosen resource (one, or a cluster — the brush). Tap a resource to select it. Nothing needs a drag; the number keys press the numbered buttons; Escape clears the selection.

Say or type (the 🎤 button listens in Chrome):

  • scatter 12 berries near the beach · plant 15 firs on the twin hills radius 12 · put a palm at the surf bay
  • make the north ridge gold-rich · make the volcano stone-rich · sprinkle some stone west of the volcano
  • select 7 (or just 7) · move 7 to the beach · move 7 north 10 · delete 7 · delete all gold · clear the volcano
  • richer 7 · poorer 7 · set 7 richness 80% · 7 amount 3 · make the beach berries richer (cluster names work)
  • berries / fir / gold… picks the type · cluster / single · size 12 · radius 10
  • look at the volcano · overview · top · zoom in · turn left · place names
  • save · save as berry tutorial · load berry-tutorial · new · play · undo · button 14
